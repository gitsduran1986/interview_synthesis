# Implementation & Integration — Expert 3

*Head of IT Operations at Solara Renewables (2020 - present)*

---
**AI Interviewer** (00:09:50)

Let's talk implementation. What was your rollout experience like, and what were the biggest challenges?

**Expert 3** (00:10:00)

Like I said, about ten weeks from contract to go-live. The biggest challenge honestly wasn't the platform - it was getting field staff to actually use tickets instead of texting or calling me directly. We had to do a real change management push, incentivize adoption, that kind of thing.

**AI Interviewer** (00:10:30)

How did Freshservice integrate with your Azure AD and Intune environment?

**Expert 3** (00:10:38)

Azure AD sync for user provisioning was straightforward, native integration, took maybe a day to configure. Intune was a bit more work - we wanted device data flowing into the asset CMDB, and we ended up using a combination of the native integration plus some manual scripting to get certain custom fields to map correctly.

**AI Interviewer** (00:11:15)

Did you use any of Freshservice's built-in integration marketplace, or was it mostly custom work?

**Expert 3** (00:11:23)

Mostly the marketplace apps for the core stuff - Azure AD, Slack for notifications, a couple of monitoring tool integrations. The custom scripting was really just for those extra Intune fields I mentioned.
