# Current Environment — Expert 2

*Director, IT Service Management at Life Sciences Firm(2021 - present)*

---
**AI Interviewer** (00:01:35)

Thanks, Priya, that's helpful context. Let's start with your current environment. Can you walk me through how Calloway uses BMC Helix and which modules you have deployed?

**Expert 2** (00:01:47)

Sure. We're on BMC Helix ITSM, cloud-hosted, and we use Incident, Problem, Change, Service Request Management, Knowledge, and the CMDB. We also use Helix Digital Workplace as the front end for employees. We don't have the full AIOps suite turned on yet - that's something we're evaluating. We handle somewhere around 60,000 tickets a year across roughly 2,200 employees and about a dozen manufacturing and lab sites.

**AI Interviewer** (00:02:34)

That's a solid deployment. What drove the decision to move off the legacy on-prem tool in the first place?

**Expert 2** (00:02:43)

A few things. The legacy tool - an old ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus instance - just wasn't scaling. It was slow, the reporting was weak, and it didn't have a real CMDB, so we had no reliable asset inventory for our lab equipment or IT hardware. We were also getting pressure from Quality and Regulatory to have better audit trails on changes, since a lot of our systems touch GxP-relevant processes. So it was really scalability, asset visibility, and compliance-grade change control.

**AI Interviewer** (00:03:28)

Were those goals tied to a larger digital transformation initiative, or was it mostly an IT-driven project?

**Expert 2** (00:03:36)

It started as an IT-driven project, honestly. I inherited the legacy tool and it was falling apart - outages, tickets getting lost. Once I built the business case around compliance risk, it got elevated, and our VP of IT and our Head of Quality co-sponsored it. So it became bigger than just an IT refresh, but it wasn't part of some grand ERP-style transformation program.

**AI Interviewer** (00:04:10)

Understood. You mentioned compliance-grade change control. What specifically does BMC Helix give you there that the old tool didn't?

**Expert 2** (00:04:20)

The big one is the change approval workflow tied to GxP flags. When someone opens a change, they tag whether it's GxP-impacting, and that routes it through Quality for review and electronic signature before it can move to implementation. We also get a full audit trail - who approved what, when, what the change actually was - which is exactly what our auditors want to see. The old tool had none of that; changes were tracked in email half the time.
