# Current Environment — Expert 3

*Head of IT Operations at Solara Renewables (2020 - present)*

---
**AI Interviewer** (00:01:35)

Thanks for that background. Can you walk me through how Solara uses Freshservice today and which modules you have deployed?

**Expert 3** (00:01:44)

We use Incident, Service Request, Change, Problem, Asset Management, and we recently turned on the Project module for some larger infrastructure rollouts. We're fully cloud, obviously - Freshservice doesn't really do on-prem. We handle somewhere around 18,000 tickets a year across about 450 employees, plus a rotating pool of field contractors who submit requests through email-to-ticket.

**AI Interviewer** (00:02:20)

What drove the original decision to formalize an ITSM platform rather than continuing with shared inboxes?

**Expert 3** (00:02:29)

Honestly, it was chaos. Nothing was tracked, nothing had an SLA, and when we started scaling up project development, leadership wanted actual metrics - how fast we're resolving things, how many devices we have deployed in the field, that kind of thing. We also started doing SOC 2 work for some of our financing partners, and having zero audit trail on IT changes was a real gap.

**AI Interviewer** (00:03:00)

Was that tied to a broader strategic initiative, or mostly IT-driven?

**Expert 3** (00:03:07)

Mostly IT and security-driven at first. It became more strategic once the SOC 2 requirement came from our CFO's office - that got leadership attention pretty fast.
