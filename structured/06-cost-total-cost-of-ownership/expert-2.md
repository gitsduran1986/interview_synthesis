*Director, IT Service Management at Life Sciences Firm(2021 - present)*

**AI Interviewer** (00:16:20)

Let's move to cost. How did BMC Helix's total cost of ownership compare to what you initially budgeted?

**Expert 2** (00:16:29)

We came in roughly 15% over our initial budget, mostly because of the SAP integration work I mentioned, plus some additional Digital Workplace licenses we ended up needing that we hadn't originally scoped for all of our site-level staff.

**AI Interviewer** (00:16:52)

Can you give me a rough sense of per-user pricing, or how it compared to the alternatives you evaluated?

**Expert 2** (00:17:00)

If I put BMC at roughly sixty dollars a user per month blended across our license tiers, ServiceNow's quote came in closer to one-sixty for a comparable module set, Ivanti was around seventy, and Freshservice was in the thirty to thirty-five dollar range, but again, with less compliance depth.

**AI Interviewer** (00:17:35)

Were there any licensing surprises, modules or features you assumed were included that turned out to be add-ons?

**Expert 2** (00:17:44)

Yes - the AIOps and predictive analytics add-on was not included in our base package, and neither was the full Digital Workplace mobile experience; that was a separate SKU we had to add mid-implementation once we realized site staff needed mobile ticket submission.

**AI Interviewer** (00:18:10)

How has renewal pricing trended since your initial contract?

**Expert 2** (00:18:16)

We're two renewal cycles in now, and increases have been in the four to six percent range each year, which has been manageable.
