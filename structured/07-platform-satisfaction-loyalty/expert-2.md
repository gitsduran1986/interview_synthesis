**AI Interviewer** (00:19:45)

**AI Interviewer** (00:20:15)

Can you give a specific example of when one of those weaknesses caused a real business impact?

**Expert 2** (00:20:23)

The SAP integration delay I mentioned - that pushed our go-live back almost two months from the original plan, and during that gap we were running dual systems, which created confusion about where certain tickets should be logged.

**AI Interviewer** (00:20:45)

Was that more a platform limitation or an implementation partner issue?

**Expert 2** (00:20:51)

A bit of both. Our SAP configuration is unusual enough that even a bigger partner ecosystem might not have had an off-the-shelf answer, but I do think BMC could have flagged that risk earlier in the sales process instead of us discovering it mid-implementation.
