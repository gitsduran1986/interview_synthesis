A 9. For our size and use case, it's been a really good fit.

**AI Interviewer** (00:14:24)

What do you see as Freshservice's biggest strengths?

**Expert 3** (00:14:29)

Ease of use, fast implementation, solid asset management for the price, and genuinely good customer support when we've had issues.

**AI Interviewer** (00:14:42)

And its biggest weaknesses?

**Expert 3** (00:14:46)

The automation builder getting clunky at complexity, renewal price creep, and I'd say reporting could be more flexible - we've had to export to Excel more than I'd like for certain board-level reports.

**AI Interviewer** (00:15:05)

Can you give a specific example of when one of those weaknesses caused a real business impact?

**Expert 3** (00:15:13)

The reporting limitation, mainly. When our CFO's office wanted a specific SOC 2 audit trail report in a particular format, we ended up building it manually in Excel every quarter rather than pulling it natively, which is just wasted time.

**AI Interviewer** (00:15:38)

Was that a platform limitation, or something that better configuration could solve?

**Expert 3** (00:15:44)

A bit of both - Freshservice's native reporting is decent for standard use cases, but for a very specific custom compliance format, it just doesn't flex enough without going into their analytics add-on, which we haven't purchased.
