# Switching Dynamics — Expert 3

*Head of IT Operations at Solara Renewables (2020 - present)*

---
**AI Interviewer** (00:16:10)

How difficult would it be for Solara to migrate away from Freshservice if leadership decided to?

**Expert 3** (00:16:18)

I'd put it around a 4 out of 10. We're not deeply entangled - the integrations are fairly standard, and our data model isn't overly customized. It'd be a project, but not a nightmare.

**AI Interviewer** (00:16:38)

What would trigger you to seriously evaluate switching?

**Expert 3** (00:16:44)

If we scaled significantly, say tripled in size, I'd want to re-evaluate whether Freshservice's automation and reporting could keep up, or whether something like ServiceNow would finally make sense. Also a serious support quality drop would do it.

**AI Interviewer** (00:17:05)

If you were to switch, which alternative would you look at first?

**Expert 3** (00:17:11)

Probably ServiceNow, just because if we're big enough to justify switching costs, I'd want the platform with the most long-term headroom.
