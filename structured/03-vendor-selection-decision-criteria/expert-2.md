# Vendor Selection & Decision Criteria — Expert 2

*Director, IT Service Management at Life Sciences Firm(2021 - present)*

---
**AI Interviewer** (00:05:10)

That makes sense. Let's move to how you selected BMC Helix. What alternatives were you evaluating at the time?

**Expert 2** (00:05:19)

We looked at ServiceNow, Ivanti Neurons for ITSM, and Freshservice. We also briefly looked at Jira Service Management but ruled it out pretty early - it just didn't have the depth we needed for CMDB and compliance workflows.

**AI Interviewer** (00:05:38)

What were your top purchasing criteria in that evaluation?

**Expert 2** (00:05:44)

Cost was definitely near the top, just given our size - we're not a $40 billion company, we're a few hundred million in revenue. Beyond cost, it was implementation speed, how well it could support GxP-style approval workflows out of the box, integration with our existing systems like SAP and Workday, and honestly, how painful the admin experience would be for a small team, since I only have three people supporting the platform.

**AI Interviewer** (00:06:22)

Could you rank your top five in strict priority order?

**Expert 2** (00:06:28)

Let me think. First would be total cost of ownership. Second, out-of-the-box support for compliance workflows. Third, implementation speed. Fourth, integration capability. And fifth, ease of administration for a small team.

**AI Interviewer** (00:07:00)

Great - TCO, compliance workflow support, implementation speed, integration, and ease of admin. Focusing on your number one, TCO, what evidence did vendors provide to prove their pricing claims?

**Expert 2** (00:07:14)

We asked all four vendors for detailed multi-year quotes, not just year-one pricing, because we'd heard stories about year-two and year-three increases. ServiceNow's rep was actually pretty transparent about the escalation, Ivanti gave us a three-year lock, and BMC gave us a similar multi-year commitment. Freshservice was cheapest but their compliance workflow story was thinner, so it dropped down our list fast.

**AI Interviewer** (00:08:00)

What ultimately tipped the decision toward BMC Helix over ServiceNow and Ivanti?

**Expert 2** (00:08:09)

Honestly, it came down to price relative to capability. ServiceNow was excellent but it was almost triple our BMC quote for a comparable module set, and for our size, that wasn't defensible internally. Ivanti was close in price to BMC, but their compliance and change-approval workflows needed more custom configuration to meet our GxP requirements. BMC had more of that baked in already from working with other pharma and med device customers. So BMC gave us the best balance of cost and compliance readiness.
