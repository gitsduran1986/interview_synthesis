# Vendor Selection & Decision Criteria — Expert 3

*Head of IT Operations at Solara Renewables (2020 - present)*

---
**AI Interviewer** (00:03:35)

Let's talk about how you selected Freshservice. What alternatives did you evaluate?

**Expert 3** (00:03:43)

We looked at ServiceNow, Ivanti since I had experience with it, Zendesk, and Freshservice. We ruled out ServiceNow pretty quickly on cost; it felt like way more platform than we needed at our size.

**AI Interviewer** (00:04:05)

What were your top purchasing criteria?

**Expert 3** (00:04:10)

Cost was number one, no question, given our size. After that, speed of implementation, ease of use for a non-technical field workforce, asset management capability since we track a lot of hardware across remote sites, and integration with our existing tools, mainly Azure AD and Intune.

**AI Interviewer** (00:04:45)

Could you rank your top five in strict priority order?

**Expert 3** (00:04:50)

First, cost. Second, ease of use. Third, implementation speed. Fourth, asset management depth. Fifth, integration with Azure AD and Intune.

**AI Interviewer** (00:05:15)

What ultimately tipped the decision toward Freshservice over Zendesk and Ivanti?

**Expert 3** (00:05:24)

Zendesk felt built more for customer support than internal IT - the asset management piece was weak. Ivanti I already knew from my last job, and honestly it wasn't a great memory - clunky admin, slow to configure. Freshservice hit the sweet spot: modern interface, built-in asset management with a discovery agent, and a price point that made sense for us.
