# Competitive Comparison — Expert 2

*Director, IT Service Management at Life Sciences Firm(2021 - present)*

---
**AI Interviewer** (00:09:00)

That's a clear rationale. Now let's do a direct comparison across your criteria. On a 1 to 10 scale, how would you rate BMC Helix, ServiceNow, Ivanti, and Freshservice on integration capability?

**Expert 2** (00:09:15)

Integration - I'd give ServiceNow a 9, BMC an 8, Ivanti a 7, and Freshservice a 6. ServiceNow's ecosystem of connectors is just bigger.

**AI Interviewer** (00:09:35)

And on total cost of ownership?

**Expert 2** (00:09:39)

TCO - flip it around. Freshservice would be a 9, BMC an 8, Ivanti a 7, and ServiceNow a 5, just given how expensive it gets at scale.

**AI Interviewer** (00:09:58)

How about ease of administration?

**Expert 2** (00:10:03)

Freshservice is probably the easiest, I'd say 9. BMC I'd give a 7 - it's manageable for my small team but there's a learning curve. Ivanti is similar, maybe a 7. ServiceNow is powerful but complex, I'd put it at a 6 for a team our size.

**AI Interviewer** (00:10:30)

And compliance workflow support specifically?

**Expert 2** (00:10:35)

BMC I'd rate an 8 for us specifically, because of the pharma-specific templates they had available. ServiceNow is probably a 9 in raw capability, but you have to build a lot of it yourself. Ivanti is a 6 - doable, but more custom work. Freshservice is weaker here, maybe a 4; it's really built more for lighter-weight IT support use cases.

**AI Interviewer** (00:11:10)

Where do you see the smallest gap between BMC Helix and its closest competitor?

**Expert 2** (00:11:17)

Probably Ivanti. They're close on cost and on general ITSM functionality. Where BMC pulled ahead was specifically in how much pre-built compliance content they had for regulated industries.

**AI Interviewer** (00:11:40)

Other experts we've spoken with have said BMC's biggest vulnerability is brand visibility and partner ecosystem size compared to ServiceNow, that ServiceNow just has more integrators and community content available. Does that match your experience?

**Expert 2** (00:11:58)

Yes, that's fair. When we needed a specialized integration, our BMC partner pool was smaller, and it sometimes took longer to find someone who'd done exactly what we needed. With ServiceNow, there's just a much bigger community and more integrators to choose from.

**AI Interviewer** (00:12:25)

Given that, where do you feel BMC Helix has a genuine edge over ServiceNow beyond price?

**Expert 2** (00:12:33)

For us specifically, the out-of-the-box compliance templates and the fact that BMC's account team had deep pharma and med device experience. They understood our GxP language without us having to explain it from scratch, which sped up implementation.
