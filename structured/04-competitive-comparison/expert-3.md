# Competitive Comparison — Expert 3

*Head of IT Operations at Solara Renewables (2020 - present)*

---
**AI Interviewer** (00:06:00)

Let's do a direct comparison. On a 1 to 10 scale, how would you rate Freshservice, ServiceNow, Ivanti, and Zendesk on ease of use?

**Expert 3** (00:06:12)

Freshservice, I'd give a 9. Zendesk's also strong there, an 8. ServiceNow I'd put at a 6 - powerful but a lot to configure. Ivanti, from my prior experience, maybe a 5.

**AI Interviewer** (00:06:35)

And on cost?

**Expert 3** (00:06:38)

Freshservice a 9, Zendesk an 8, Ivanti a 7, ServiceNow a 4 for a company our size.

**AI Interviewer** (00:06:55)

Asset management depth?

**Expert 3** (00:07:00)

ServiceNow's probably the strongest on paper, I'd give it a 9, but it's overkill for us. Freshservice I'd say a 7 - solid discovery agent, good enough for our needs. Ivanti's asset piece was actually decent, maybe a 7 as well. Zendesk is weak here, a 3.

**AI Interviewer** (00:07:30)

And integration capability with tools like Azure AD and Intune?

**Expert 3** (00:07:36)

Freshservice, an 8 - the Azure AD sync was pretty painless. ServiceNow's probably a 9 in raw capability. Ivanti was a 6 in my experience, lots of manual config. Zendesk, maybe a 5.

**AI Interviewer** (00:08:00)

Where do you see the smallest gap between Freshservice and its closest competitor?

**Expert 3** (00:08:08)

Zendesk, probably, on ease of use and general user experience. Where Freshservice pulls ahead is asset management and the IT-specific workflows - ITIL processes that Zendesk just isn't built around.

**AI Interviewer** (00:08:35)

Other experts have told us Freshservice's biggest vulnerability is automation depth at scale, that it can hit limits once ticket volume or workflow complexity grows significantly. Does that match your experience?

**Expert 3** (00:08:50)

Yeah, that's fair. We're starting to feel some of that as we've added more complex approval chains for the Project module. It's not a hard wall, but you notice the automation builder gets clunky once you're chaining a lot of conditional logic.

**AI Interviewer** (00:09:15)

Given that, where do you feel Freshservice still has a genuine edge over ServiceNow?

**Expert 3** (00:09:22)

Cost, hands down, and time to value. We were live in about ten weeks. I don't think we'd have been live on ServiceNow in under six months, and it would've cost multiples of what we paid.
